## 1. Navy sole-sources more KPMG AI and data work, citing audit deadlines

**DEPT OF THE NAVY (DEPT OF DEFENSE) · KPMG · 10 U.S.C. 3406(c)(5)**  
*Posted 2026-10-07, award N0018922DZ015, “Financial Management Data Transformation, Data Analytics and Data Engineering Services”*

The Navy plans a cost-plus-fixed-fee modification to KPMG's sole-source task order for a rapid response team, a budget orchestration system and Foundry data objects. The document ties the urgency to Congress's FY28 auditability mandate and Defense Secretary memo language.

> “In the FY2024 National Defense Authorization Act, Congress mandated that all components of the DoW be auditable by FY28.”  
> — JEFO redact_Redacted.pdf, p. 3 (machine-transcribed page)

> “Contract number N0018926FZ108 was awarded on a sole source basis to KPMG, a large business; the existing contract expires on 13 April 2028.”  
> — JEFO redact_Redacted.pdf, p. 1 (machine-transcribed page)

## 2. ICE sole-sources Hootsuite social media platform with AI and visual recognition

**US IMMIGRATION AND CUSTOMS ENFORCEMENT (HOMELAND SECURITY, DEPARTMENT OF) · Hootsuite Inc. · FAR 8.401(b)**  
*Posted 2026-10-08, award 70RTAC26A00000011, “Social Media Management Support Services”*

ICE's Office of Public Affairs wants Hootsuite licenses through a Carahsoft BPA. The justification cites proprietary AI, image and video recognition, and licensed access to large-scale social data.

> “The platform includes image and video recognition that detects logos, scenes, objects, and other visual indicators in social media content.”  
> — 1.5.1-BNJ_Redacted.pdf, p. 2

> “Messages are automatically triaged by urgency, routed to the appropriate team or field office, and tracked with assignment records showing who received the item and when.”  
> — 1.5.1-BNJ_Redacted.pdf, p. 2
