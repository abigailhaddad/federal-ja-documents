**DEPT OF THE NAVY (DEPT OF DEFENSE) · Serco, Inc · 10 U.S.C. 3204(a)(1)-(2) as implemented by FAR 6.103-1 and FAR 6.103-2**  
*Posted 2026-10-20, award N0018926CD020, “Collective Joint Training Bridge Contract”*

## 5. NASA sole-sources $11.89M autonomy modules; a correction admits a competitor's response was missed

**NATIONAL AERONAUTICS AND SPACE ADMINISTRATION · EraDrive, Inc. · $11,890,000**  
*Posted 2026-10-20, award 80ARC026C0006, “Justification for Other Than Full and Open Competition: Spacecraft Optical Navigation and Autonomy Modules (Contract 80ARC026C0006)”*
